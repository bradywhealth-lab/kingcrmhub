About Brady Wilson

By day I sell final expense life insurance — on the phone, to real people, one honest conversation at a time. I know exactly what a sales day looks like: the offers you send, the replies you chase, the calls you book, the proposals you write, and the very first payment that makes it real.

That’s why the Side Hustle Launch Pad exists. I got tired of planners that make you feel busy while the market stays quiet. Mine doesn’t track habits. It tracks proof — and every week it forces one decision: stop, repair, test, or double down.

The Big Lines Coloring Series came from the same impatience. I watched a two-year-old try to color inside thin, tiny lines and it broke my heart a little. So I built the opposite: one big picture per page, extra-thick outlines, single-sided printing. The rule never changed — if a little hand can’t finish it, it doesn’t ship.

I publish under my own name because I stand behind every page count and every claim. Life’s short. Use tools that work.

— Brady Wilson
